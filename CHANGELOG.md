# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Open the current stage from a decision card and return focus to the same card when leaving the workspace. Active concern cards can open the corresponding concern in the group workspace.
- Navigate available sections from the color-coded decision journey. The decision header keeps the group, initiative and current wording visible, and closed decisions show the recorded result and available implementation details.
- Use neutral surfaces that follow the Nextcloud theme, with stage colors reserved for the decision journey.
- Preserve existing connections, grants, confirmation rules and pending operations during an ordinary update.

## 0.3.0 — public availability

- Connect existing Deferendum accounts from compatible public HTTPS Nextcloud instances after ownership proof and explicit per-member consent.
- Clarify setup and availability in Help and all five interface languages.
- Preserve the app ID, local registration, existing connections, settings and pending operations during an ordinary update.
- Existing grants retain their selected groups, permissions and expiry.


## 0.3.0-beta.1 — 2026-10-16

- Bring the Deferendum PWA visual language to the Nextcloud workspace: matte glass, subtle aurora colors, consistent icons and responsive navigation.
- Group guide, 20 searchable FAQ entries, feedback and security contacts in Help. Add a replayable four-step tour.
- Separate workspace, Help and Settings. Keep connection and registration controls out of the participation view.
- Apply list filters explicitly and return keyboard focus to their disclosure.
- Show a live deadline countdown without changing source phase or eligibility.
- Collapse secondary forms and context; put the ballot first during voting and keep its review confirmation intact.
- Keep the 0.2.4 lifecycle correction. No disable/uninstall hook or credential migration is added. Existing connections, grants and preferences are retained by an ordinary app update.
- Preserve the command, permission, immutable-ballot and uncertain-outcome recovery contracts.

This is a beta connector. The hosted participation service remains limited to the existing private fictitious-data pilot. Installation does not make that service generally available. This release is not a declaration of completed production or volunteer acceptance.
